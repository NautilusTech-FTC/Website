Science Geek Out Fest at the Beaverton City Library

Showing local families what we do

We went to the annual Science Geek Out Fest at BCL Main to allow local kids to drive our robot and learn about what we do. We had a small field setup and a goal in the corner and allowed interested kids to drive our robot, and we set up a table with past season elements, our engineering portfolio, links to important information, and the state championship stream that we were in. We even used our small triangle robot to go around the festival and get kids excited about driving our larger robot at our booth, and this allowed us to reach a very large percentage of the people who came to this library-run event.

The Summer STEM Fair

Bringing STEM to children in our neighborhood

We set up an event in the cul-de-sac in front of our lab for kids in the neighborhood to learn about robotics and what we do. Those who came had the chance to drive our Decode competition robot, build and program LEGO Spike Prime robots, and even learn how to make autonomous pathways using the Pedro Pathing visualization software, do some basic Java programming on Android Studio, and CAD and 3D print their own keychains in OnShape. If people got a stamp at every station on their stamp card, they would get a prize at our prize booth, and because of that many of the people who came ended up visiting every single station, maximizing the amount that they learned and experienced at our event.

The Whitford Middle School Science Fair

Recruitment and outreach event with 20790 Hawkbot1cs

We went to the Whitford Middle School science fair with Hawkbot1cs to try to recruit new members from the school and spread information about robotics opportunities in general. We each brought our competition robots from the season and Hawkbot1cs brought their field, we also both brought our own displays and we brought our tent so our setup would be visible from as many places in the room as possible. We handed out a large number of flyers about recruitment and ORTOP and FTC and general, making sure the students of the school new about the opportunities avaliable if they were interested in pursuing robotics.

Jake

Applied Engineering

We helped Jake by making custom parts for his van. From this we learned how to use the engineering design process for real-world custom projects with unique constraints. This increased our attention to detail and process while working on our own robot, and helped Jake with work he couldn't do alone.

County Fair

Bringing STEM to Young Fairgoers with 12599 Overcharged

We set joined two other teams at the Clark County Fair in Washington, and we showed off our robot and spread info about FIRST and ORTOP. We learned the best ways to market our team while broadcasting STEM learning to the community in collaboration with others. This inspired young kids to involve themselves in robotics and other STEM fields and gave them resources to easily do so.

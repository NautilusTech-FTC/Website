Youth Robotics Summer STEM Fair

June 13th, 10:00am to 4:00pm

Come visit our team and learn about robotics through demos and hands-on projects! All ages are welcome, with activities geared towards elemetary and middle school age kids.

Activities include:

  1. Learning to build FIRST Lego League (FLL) robots
  2. Learning to program FLL robots with block coding
  3. LEGO play area
  4. Snack Booth
  5. Demonstrations of robots from last year's FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) season DECODE
  6. Hands-on Computer-Aided Design Demonstrations
  7. Hands-on programming demonstrations in Java
  8. Hands-on autonomous presentations using Pedro Pathing visualisation software
  9. Prizes!

Attendence is free, so make sure to come visit us at the corner of SW Bridletrail Ave and SW Stirrup St!

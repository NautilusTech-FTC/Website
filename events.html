Science Geek Out Fest at Beaverton City Library Main

Sunday July 19th

Come meet our team and learn about our robot at this library-run event! The event will also have many other local STEM organizations, so it would be a great place to wander around and learn for the whole family. The event will be from 2:00pm to 4:00pm at the main library building at 12375 sw Fifth St, Beaverton OR. Visit this link for the full details: wccls.bibliocommons.com/events/6a303cdb75b1e22800813c47

We will be bringing our competition robot, our triangle bot, game elements, and many more fun things! So if that sounds interesting to you, come check it out! We'd love to show you our robot in person!

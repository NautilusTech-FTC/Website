Silicon Forest League Scrimmage

October 24th at Hillsboro High School, time coming soon

Come see us compete at our first event of the season! This league scrimmage doesn't count for points for the rest of the season, it's just a more casual event for the teams in our league to practice with their robots and get a feel for the game. The event will be at Hillsboro High School, and unlike all of the other events, has a hard end time since getting all the scheduled matches in does not matter. Watch the matches in-person or livestreamed online at this link which will be put here once it is put out on YouTube. After the event, you can read about what happened on the This Year's Season page of this website. See our league's teams' first steps in this season!

Silicon Forest League Meet 1

November 14th at Hillsboro High School, time coming soon

This is the first event this season that counts for points! Come to watch teams trying their hardest to best each other with innovative robot desgins that have been worked on for a month since their first shot at competing. This event, like all league meets, will be at Hillsboro High School if you would like to watch in person, or at this link (added once it goes live on YouTube) if you'd prefer to watch remotely. After the event, you can read about what happened on the This Year's Season page of this website. Be sure to watch to experience the begining of the four-meet-long league competition!

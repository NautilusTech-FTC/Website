Teaser at the FIRST Finale

At the FIRST finale, they released the name of next year's season: BIOBUZZ, as well as the scoring elements: 2.9in pickleball-shaped Pollen. In addition, four companies have released the bases to their starter bots for this season, and FIRST has released lesson plans to help teams get started. We have been pouring over these resources as a team, as we are very excited for next year's season. We are also planning on participating in many of the lesson plans to prepare for next season and train new members. Here are all of the resources out so far that we know of:

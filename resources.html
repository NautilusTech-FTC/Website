FIRST

FIRST Robotics is a non-profit organization that gives kids of grades K-12 opportunities to participate in many levels of robotics programs while teaching skills of innovation, impact, and teamwork. They have inspired 785,000+ young individuals from 110 countries worldwide. Check out more about them at https://www.firstinspires.org/

ORTOP

ORTOP is a volunteer-based organization that helps to bring FIRST to the local Oregon communities. They make all FIRST events possible in Oregon. You can learn more about them here at https://ortop.org/

LEGO Education

Lego not only makes fun building kits, but it also has a full built out curriculum where you can learn to build, code, and create functional robots. Once you buy a kit of their hardware, you have dozens of pre-made lessons that can teach kids of all ages how to be engineers. Our team used this software to first develop our skills as an award winning FIRST Lego League team. If you are at all interested, you can get started at https://education.lego.com/en-us/
